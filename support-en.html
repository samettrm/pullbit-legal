Frequently asked questions

How do I download?

Copy the link from the post's share menu and open PULLBIT; tap the paste button on the home screen. You can also choose PULLBIT from the Share menu of another app. When the preview opens, pick the items and quality and tap "Download".

Where are my downloads?

In the Library tab of the app. Open an item and use "Save to Photos" or "Save to Files" to export it to your device's gallery or files.

My download stopped halfway. Does it start over?

The app tries to resume where it left off. If the source does not support that, or the file changed in the meantime, the download starts over.

Do I need an account?

No. PULLBIT has no user accounts.

How do I turn off usage statistics?

Settings › Privacy › "Share usage statistics".

How do I delete my data?

You can delete library items in the app. Removing the app deletes all PULLBIT data on the device. Details: Privacy Policy.

When you write to us

To help us solve the problem faster, include: your device and operating system version, the app version (Settings › About) and the title of the message the app showed. Include the link you tried to open only if you are willing to share it.
