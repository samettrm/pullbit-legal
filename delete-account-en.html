Deleting your PULLBIT account

Effective date
03-10-2026
App
PULLBIT (BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş.)
Contact
bsdigitalapp@gmail.com

An account is optional in PULLBIT. If you signed in with your Apple or Google account, you can delete your account and the data associated with it in the following ways.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open PULLBIT and go to the Settings tab.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete account permanently.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you signed in with Apple, Apple may ask you to confirm once more.

If the app is no longer on your device

  • You can reinstall the app, sign in with the same account and follow the steps above.
  • Your account is also deleted if you do nothing: an account that is not used for 180 days is deleted from our server automatically.

We cannot find your account from an e-mail request: we do not store your name or your e-mail address; all we hold is an irreversible digest of the account number your sign-in provider gives us. For questions: bsdigitalapp@gmail.com.

The record at Apple and Google

Deleting your account may not remove the "signed in with this app" record in your Apple or Google account. You can remove it yourself:

  • Apple: on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name and choose PULLBIT under "Sign in with Apple".
  • Google: Google Account › Third-party apps and services.
